Architectural Description & Essence

Heritage Craft

Perched graciously along the tranquil rim of world-renowned Punnamada Lake, Asset Nilaavu stands as an exceptional homage to traditional Kerala vernacular re-imagined through contemporary glass and timber lines. Every room is deliberately engineered to face east-northeast, greeting every sunrise with panoramic reflections off the legendary Nehru Trophy boat race waters.

Interiors are adorned with hand-planed Nilambur teak door frames, Italian Statuario marble in living areas, and expansive floor-to-ceiling glass façades with high acoustic resistance. Located a leisurely 2-minute stroll from the landmark Ramada Resort by Wyndham, residents enjoy an isolated haven coupled with direct marina and luxury hospitality access.
